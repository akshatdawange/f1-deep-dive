Introduction

Formula One (F1) is the highest class of worldwide racing for open-wheel single-seater formula racing cars sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The FIA Formula One World Championship has been one of the world's premier forms of motorsport since its inaugural running in 1950 and is often considered to be the pinnacle of motorsport. The word formula in the name refers to the set of rules all participant cars must follow. A Formula One season consists of a series of races, known as Grands Prix. Grands Prix take place in multiple countries and continents on either purpose-built circuits or closed roads.

For the scope of this assessment, I have just considered the season of 2024 as it is the most recently completed season. The 2025 Championship season is still in progress.

A record-breaking 24 Grands Prix were scheduled for the 2024 Formula 1 season, though ultimately 23 races were held. The Chinese Grand Prix returned for the first time since 2019, allowing Zhou Guanyu to compete in his home race in April. Imola also rejoined the calendar after being cancelled in 2023 due to severe flooding. There were no entirely new races in 2024, with recent additions like Qatar, Miami, and Las Vegas already established. Notably, three races were held on Saturdays—Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and Las Vegas—following the format introduced in 2023. The Japanese Grand Prix moved to an earlier slot in April, and the season concluded in early December in Abu Dhabi.

Constructors

In terms of Formula 1, a team is called as a constructor. Every constructor consists of two drivers racing for that team. The first Formula 1 season saw 30 teams contest. With time, there have been teams which have enterred and left the sport at certain point in time. There have been various teams that have been rebranded as well i.e. they have been sold to different owners or brands. Alongside the drivers championship, there is a constructor championship as well which is fought between the teams. The pair of drivers together contribute to the scores of the team. For example, in a team, if a driver scores 8 points and the other driver scores 25 points in a particular race, the total score of the team for that race is calculated as 25 + 8 = 33 points for that race. These points keep accumulating and the team with the most points in the end of the season is crowned as the champion.

In the 2024 season, McLaren were crowned as the season winners with 666 points followed closely by Ferrari standing at 652 points. The 10 teams who were a part of the 2024 season are shown on the left.

The teams have been the same for the 2025 season, with driver swaps happening early in the season. 2 new teams are set to join the grid for the 2026 season. However, Audi will be replacing the existing Kick Sauber Team.

Over the years, constructors have not only competed for race wins but have also become engineering powerhouses, pushing the boundaries of automotive innovation. Advancements in aerodynamics, hybrid engines, and pit-stop strategies all stem from the relentless pursuit of performance. These innovations often trickle down to consumer vehicles, making Formula 1 a key contributor to global automotive progress.

Drivers and Team Analysis

This section is a critical part in this web page as we can get a detailed information regarding the performance of different teams, drivers, how teams have been successful over the years and how the drivers have contributed to their success. Another crucial information is how have the drivers been related to one another.

The Driver Network Analysis shows how the drivers relation has been with teammates over the years.

The interaction in this section are as follows:

  1. Select Year from the dropdown. Default set to 2000
  2. Once the year is selected, the next dropdown appears which helps in selecting the team.
  3. After the team is selected, the driver points contribution for that season is showed in the form of the bar chat. The number in the center is the total points for the team.
  4. The team which you select, is the one which will be highlighted on the team points chart for better comparison
  5. When you click the bar of any driver, you can view the driver teammate network.
  6. Additionally, there is a hover effect on the nodes to show more in detail statistics.
